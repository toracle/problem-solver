=== Session Facilitation Notes

* ★ Session 1 is an awareness session. Establish a "no right or wrong" atmosphere first.
* ★ When writing Solution Space items on the board after the "30-second experiment," do not criticize them. Let participants discover together "why is this in the Solution Space."
* ★ Make it clear that Presumptive Architecture is not bad. The difference between "using it knowing it's an assumption" and "using it thinking it's a fact."
* ★ For groups that generated few Problem Space questions, ask "why didn't more come up?" The volume comparison itself is learning material.

=== 1. Opening Provocation (10 minutes) — "The 30-Second Experiment"

Without slides or preamble, distribute paper and say:

[quote]
____
"Minjun just said this:
____
[quote]
____
'Can you beef up the team collaboration features? Notifications and task assignment. Demo next month.'
____
[quote]
____
In the next 30 seconds, write down everything that comes to mind."
____

After 30 seconds, ask a few people to read what they wrote.

_Typical responses:_
----
"Real-time notifications with WebSocket"
"If we use Kafka for event processing"
"Create a separate notifications table"
"Firebase Cloud Messaging"
"Add an assignee column for task assignment"
----

Write these on the board and say:

[quote]
____
"Everything written here is in the Solution Space.
____
[quote]
____
Is there even one thing from the Problem Space?"
____

Brief silence. This is the entire argument of this session.

'''

=== 2. Concept Explanation (20 minutes)

_Concept 1 — Einstellung Effect (10 minutes)_

The phenomenon where familiar solutions block better ones.
This effect grows stronger with more experience.
Explicitly explain why engineers at 3–4 years are particularly at risk.

_Concept 2 — Presumptive Architecture (5 minutes)_

Every developer carries an unconscious architecture default.
This itself is not bad.
Emphasize the difference between "using it knowing it's an assumption" and "using it thinking it's fact."

_Concept 3 — Problem Space vs Solution Space (5 minutes)_

Both spaces must be visited.
Key: the transition must be _conscious_.
The problem is automatic switching.

'''
