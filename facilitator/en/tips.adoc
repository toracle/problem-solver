== Operational Tips — Overall

=== Establishing Psychological Safety

The opening provocations in this workshop ask participants to reflect on their existing habits.
If an atmosphere of criticism or "you were wrong" develops, learning closes down.
Maintain the frame: "The choice wasn't wrong — there was another way."

=== When Participants Get Stuck

The most common points where participants get stuck on worksheets:

[cols="1,1", options="header"]
|===
| Stuck point | Facilitator intervention
| Attributes column is empty in FAC | Ask "Why would it matter to know this?"
| State isn't surfacing in the Domain Model | Prompt "List all the states this concept can be in"
| R/E classification is difficult | Ask "Is there an action you can take right now to find out?"
| No analogies come to mind | First have them "abstract this problem's structure in one sentence"
| Too many risks are surfacing | Narrow with "Which of these could kill the project?"
|===


=== Managing Worksheet B

Worksheet B carries the same case through all 8 sessions.
Remind participants to "take out your Worksheet B" at the start of each session.
Participants who didn't register a case in Session 1 can register in Session 2.

=== Assignment Card Connections

Each session opens with 5 minutes where 2–3 people briefly share results from the previous session's assignment.
Keep it voluntary rather than forced, but make sure to surface interesting cases when they appear.
This sharing is what creates the workshop's sense of being grounded in real work.
