Seeing the Problem

Problem Space / Solution Space Training Workshop
"Looking at the problem longer is the way to arrive at the solution faster."

An 8-session workshop for fullstack engineers with 3–4 years of experience. It builds the muscle to recognize the habit of jumping into Solution Space the moment a requirement comes in, and to explore Problem Space deeply.

Grounded in the work of Pólya, Weinberg & Gause, Fairbanks, Cunningham, and Schön. Across the 8 sessions, we repeatedly explore a fictional startup case study, "TeamOrder."
