_Small Group Share (10 minutes)_

----
Comparison Points
──────────────────
"Did different teams find analogous cases in entirely different domains?"
  → The same problem can yield analogies from completely different places.
    If one team found hospital triage and another found TCP retransmission,
    both are valid. The principles they extracted will differ.
    List those differences on the board.

"Where did you get stuck when importing the principle?"
  → Check: were you trying to copy the solution, or import the principle?
    The former almost always ends with "that doesn't fit our situation."

"How was reading Trade-offs different from simply listing features?"
  → Bring out this question to get participants to articulate the difference
    in research perspective.
