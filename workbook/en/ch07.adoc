_Small Group Share (8 minutes)_

Comparison Points
──────────────────
"Do different teams have different top-3 risks?"
  → Same system, different risk priorities —
    what contextual difference produced that judgment?
    May be a demo perspective vs production perspective gap.

"Are there cases where PM Risk and Engineering Risk are confused?"
  → Most commonly mixed:
    "We probably can't finish in a month" → PM Risk
    "We probably can't guarantee night notification delivery" → Engineering Risk
    Point out that the techniques for each are completely different.

"Was 'no need to decide now' hard to fill in?"
  → If yes — why was it hard?
    How do you negotiate with the pressure to design everything perfectly?
    This is the core mindset shift of this session.
