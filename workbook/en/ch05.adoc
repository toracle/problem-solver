_Small Group Share (5 minutes)_

----
Comparison Points
──────────────────
"Is there an ambiguity one team classified R and another classified E?"
  → Why they saw it differently is the key.
    Was there anything classified E where action was actually possible?
    Conversely, was anything classified R that nobody actually knows?

"What was the hardest part of designing the Spike?"
  → Pinning down what "simplest" means.
    Repeatedly ask: "Can this be made even simpler?"

"Did people use the 'no decision needed right now' column?"
  → This judgment is itself an important capability.
    Moving away from the compulsion to resolve all ambiguity immediately.
----
