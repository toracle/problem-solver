_Small Group Share (8 minutes)_

Groups of 3–4 compare PART 2 results.

.Comparison Points
****
"Is there a case where different things were written in the same column?" +
-> That difference is itself an example of between-interpreter ambiguity.

"Did anyone classify an Attribute as a Function?" +
-> Invite discussion about why they classified it that way.

"Is the Constraints column the most empty?" +
-> It usually is. Constraints are the hardest to surface. +
   Ask "why didn't this one come to mind?" as a meta-question.
****

**Full Group Share (7 minutes)**

Collect each group's "most dangerous gap" onto the board.
If different groups chose different things -- why is the core of the discussion.
